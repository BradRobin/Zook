Why We Process Your Data

Legal Basis: Your explicit consent (Data Protection Act 2019, Section 30)
Purpose: Real-time security monitoring and threat detection to enhance safety

How We Process Your Data

  • All AI detection runs locally on our servers - no cloud processing
  • Camera feed analyzed in real-time, not stored unless threat detected
  • Threat recordings saved for review and validation
  • No data shared with third parties
  • No international data transfers

Data Retention

  • Video Clips: 7 days, then auto-deleted
  • False Positives: Deleted immediately after AI validation (if confidence <90%)
  • Session Metadata: 24 hours (if no threats detected)
  • User Accounts: Retained until you request deletion

Your Rights (Data Protection Act 2019)

Under Section 38-45 of the DPA 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object: Object to processing for legitimate interests
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent at any time (account deletion)

To exercise your rights: Contact privacy@zook.ai or use the Settings menu

Data Security

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Password hashing (bcrypt)
  • JWT authentication tokens (24-hour expiry)
  • Role-based access control
  • Regular security audits

Automated Decision-Making
